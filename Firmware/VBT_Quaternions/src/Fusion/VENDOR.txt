Why it is here: since firmware v3.12.0 it is the orientation filter of the
firmware (wrapped by FusionOrientation.h/.cpp, which are ODKI's own files).

Arduino compiles everything under the sketch's src/ folder recursively, so no
build setting is needed. To update: replace the files with those of a newer
release, update this note, and re-run tools/full_replay/regression.py (and the
private real-log regression) - a changed output must be explained in a
MotionTracker.cpp version note and the golden file updated deliberately.

Note on names: Fusion v1.2.x called the gyroscope bias module FusionOffset and
the rejection timeout recoveryTriggerPeriod (in samples); v1.3.x renamed them FusionBias and
rejectionTimeout (same roles).
